Your own team of AI agents.
They do the work. You make the calls.

Harman runs a swarm of AI agents on a computer you own. They research, write, code, file and follow up. When something needs a human — a decision, an approval, a password — it lands on one board on your phone, with everything you need to decide in one tap.

Get early access

Private beta. iPhone app plus a server that runs on your Mac or PC.

One board, every decision

Agents park questions and approvals on a shared board, like a team using Jira. Approve, decline, ask a follow-up or snooze. Nothing you skip is lost: it comes back on the next round.

Full context, every time

Every ask is written for someone who has never seen the work: what is needed, why, and what each choice does. You decide in seconds, not after an hour of catching up.

Runs on your machine

Your files, mail and accounts stay on hardware you control. Agents act only through the services you connect, and only with the permissions you grant.

Remembers what it learns

Work lives on cards, knowledge in notes, and procedures as skills every agent inherits. People and sessions come and go; the organisation keeps running.

One system, many roles

  • Law office — read and annotate case documents, draft letters, track deadlines.
  • Finance — read bank and card emails, flag bills and cash shortfalls, plan payoffs.
  • Personal assistant — mail, calendar, reminders and errands, with you approving anything outward-facing.
  • Engineering — agents write, test and ship code, and ask before touching anything shared.
  • Founder / CEO — a working team that turns your goals into cards and comes back only for your judgment.

How it works

  1. Install the Harman server on your Mac or PC and pair your iPhone with a QR code.
  2. Tell it your goal. Agents break it into cards and get to work.
  3. Get a push when your judgment is needed. Answer in one tap, or reply with a comment.
