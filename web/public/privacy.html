Privacy Policy

Harman — last updated 30 October 2026

Harman is self-hosted software: a team of AI agents that runs on a computer you own, plus an iPhone app that talks to it. This policy explains what data Harman handles, where it goes, and what we (the company that makes Harman) can and cannot see. "You" means the customer who installs and runs Harman.

The short version

  • The Harman server runs on your machine, and your data lives in your own Postgres database on that machine. We do not host it and cannot read it.
  • Your prompts and the data agents work on go to the AI model provider you choose, under that provider's terms.
  • We run two small services: a relay that lets your phone reach your server (end-to-end encrypted, so it cannot read your traffic) and a private update feed.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train models.

Data that stays on your machine

Everything Harman works with is stored on the computer you install it on: chats and agent transcripts, board cards, notes, files, settings, the sign-in tokens for accounts you connect, API keys for model providers, and the device tokens your phone registers for notifications. You control that machine, so you control the data: you can read it, back it up, or delete it.

The phone relay

So your phone can reach your server without opening it to the internet, both dial out to a relay we run on Cloudflare (relay.suhai.ai). After a short handshake, every message between phone and server is end-to-end encrypted (NaCl box: X25519 and XSalsa20-Poly1305). The phone learns your server's public key from the pairing QR code, so only your server can decrypt it. The relay passes along encrypted bytes it cannot read.

What the relay does handle: your server's random relay ID; a hash of your server's relay token, kept so nobody else can claim that ID; connection timing and size; and the phone's IP address, which it passes to your server so your server can limit repeated sign-in attempts. Cloudflare keeps standard request and error logs for this service. You can turn the relay off and reach your server another way, such as a private network.

Accounts you connect

You can connect accounts such as Google, Dropbox, Microsoft OneDrive, and API accounts like cloud or analytics providers. Each connection is set up by you, and its sign-in token is stored on your server, not with us. Agents use a connection only for the operations you have switched on for it. Data from these accounts is read by your server and may be sent to your chosen model provider as part of an agent's work. It is not sent to us.

For Google, Harman asks only for these permissions, and only for the services you connect:

  • Gmail (gmail.readonly, gmail.compose): search and read your mail, and write drafts. Harman does not send mail; you send a draft yourself from Gmail.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly, and calendar.events when agents may change events): read your events to plan around them, and add or move events unless you switch that off. Deleting events is off unless you turn it on. Harman never emails your guests.
  • Google Drive (drive): browse, read and save files, which appear in Harman's Files tab. Deleting files is off unless you turn it on.
  • Your Google account address (openid, email): so Harman knows which account a connection belongs to.

You can disconnect an account in Harman at any time, and revoke Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google Limited Use disclosure

Harman's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice: Google data is used only to provide the features you asked for. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train general AI models, and not read by people at [LEGAL ENTITY]. It is passed to the model provider you configure only so an agent can do the task you gave it.

AI model providers

Agents think by sending prompts to a large language model. Prompts, and whatever data an agent includes to do its job (mail, files, notes, web pages), go to the provider you configure: for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or a model you host yourself. Speech features likewise use the speech service you configure. That provider handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy, using your account or API key. Choose a provider whose terms suit your data.

Push notifications

When an agent finishes a turn or needs your decision, your server sends a notification to your iPhone through Apple Push Notification service. A notification carries a short title, a preview of up to about 300 characters (for example the first line of an agent's reply or the question it is asking), and an internal reference so tapping it opens the right screen. Apple delivers it to your device. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings.

Updates

Your server checks our private update feed (releases.suhai.ai) about once an hour and downloads new versions when they are published. Each request carries the update token issued to your installation, so the feed sees that token's use, your server's IP address, and which files it fetched. No other data from your server is sent. Updates are signed, and your server checks the signature before installing.

Usage statistics

To improve the app, your Harman web and phone clients record which screens and controls you use, and any feedback notes you write. These records are sent only to your own server and stored there. They are not sent to us.

The iPhone app

The app has no advertising or third-party analytics. It signs in to your own server, not to an account with us. Some screens load open-source display libraries (for terminals, diagrams and math) from public code CDNs, which see an ordinary download request.

This website

Our website (suhai.ai) is a static page with no analytics scripts or tracking cookies of its own. If you email us, for example to ask for early access, we keep your message and address to reply to you and to run the beta.

Retention and deletion

Data on your server stays until you delete it. Removing Harman and its database from your machine erases it. To have us delete what we hold about you (your update token, early-access emails, billing records), email [CONTACT EMAIL]. Relay and feed logs are kept by Cloudflare for its standard log period. We may keep billing records as long as the law requires.
