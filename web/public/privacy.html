Privacy Policy

Harman — last updated 8 September 2026

Harman is a personal, single-user assistant built and operated by Ram Vinoth for his own use. It is not a commercial product, it has no other users, and it does not offer accounts to the public.

Who runs this

Harman is operated by an individual. Contact: ramvinoth37@gmail.com.

What data is accessed

With the operator's explicit Google consent, Harman may access:

  • Gmail — message metadata (sender, subject, date), message bodies, and attachments, in order to summarise the operator's own inbox and surface items needing attention. It may also compose drafts and send mail at the operator's request.
  • Google Calendar — events, in order to report the operator's own schedule.

Only the operator's own Google account is ever accessed.

How data is used

Data is used solely to present the operator's own information back to him in a personal dashboard, and to carry out actions he explicitly asks for. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models, and it is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Where data is stored

Retrieved content is stored in a private database on hardware the operator physically controls. The dashboard is not exposed to the public internet; it is reachable only from the operator's own machine and private network. OAuth tokens are stored locally with restricted file permissions and are never transmitted anywhere except to Google.

Sharing

No human other than the operator has access. Message content may be sent to a language-model provider strictly to generate the operator's own summaries. No data is shared for any other purpose.

Retention and deletion

Data persists until the operator deletes it. Because the database is under his direct control, it can be erased at any time. Google access can be revoked at any moment at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends Harman's ability to read any further data.

Limited Use disclosure

Harman's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
